<h2 align="center" >
  <b><i>Design of a Long-Range Data Transmission System Using LoRa  (Long Range communication) for Soil Moisture Measurement with a short Interdigital Capacitor Structure</i></b>
</h2>

#### *Project overview*
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In this project, we develop a compact Interdigital Capacitor (IDC) using the dry film method. This capacitor is designed for soil moisture sensing, utilizing capacitance measurements that vary based on the volumetric   water content in the soil.

#### *Features & Technology*
- **Sensor Type:** Interdigital Capacitor (IDC) - Short configuration.
- **Fabrication Process:** Dry Film Lithography.
- **Application:** Agricultural IoT, Smart Irrigation.
- **Working Principle:** Capacitive sensing based on the dielectric constant variation of soil-water mixtures.

#### *Equipment & Program*
- **`LILYGO T3 (ESP32 with LoRa):`** A Microcontroller, which both calculate and transmit/receive data.
- **`Arduino IDE:`** A code compiler, writing in C and C++.
- **`Interdigital Capacitor:`** A sensor use for sensing soil capacitance.

#### *How It Works*
1. **Capacitive Sensing:** Water has a high dielectric constant compared to dry soil. As the water content in the soil changes, the overall capacitance of the sensor shifts.
2. **Data Acquisition:** The IDC sensor detects these changes and outputs a capacitance value, which can be calibrated to determine the exact volumetric water content (VWC) of the soil.
